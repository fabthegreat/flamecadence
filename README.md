## 🚀 Overview

**PaceForge** is a web-based workout designer and analytics dashboard that bridges natural language AI generation with professional training platforms like **intervals.icu**. Describe your desired training session in plain English or French, let the built-in AI forge the structure, review the interactive workout graph, and sync it directly to your training calendar.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🤖 AI Workout Generator:** Prompt the assistant to build personalized workouts tailored to your current load, recent activities, and goals using Gemini, Hugging Face, or OpenAI-compatible APIs.
* **✍️ Manual Workout Editor:** Fine-tune interval blocks, warm-ups, cool-downs, and repetition groups with precise controls for time, distance, intensity, and power units.
* **📚 Built-in Workout Library:** Instantly preview and load proven session templates (Sweet Spot, Over-Unders, VO2max intervals, and Zone 2 endurance).
* **📊 Intervals.icu Integration:** Seamlessly sync generated workouts to your calendar, pull your FTP, and view your recent training load, fitness metrics (CTL, ATL, TSB), and power curves.

---

## 🛠️ Getting Started

1. Open `paceforge.html` directly in any modern web browser.
2. Click the **⚙ Settings** button in the header.
3. Enter your **intervals.icu API key** and Athlete ID (found under your intervals.icu developer settings).
4. Choose your preferred AI provider (e.g., Gemini) and enter your API key to start generating workouts instantly!
